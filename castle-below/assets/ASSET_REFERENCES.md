# Character asset references

- `IMG_3536.JPG` is the sole identity reference for Coji's face, hair, body shape, and Cambridge uniform.
- `coji-walk-a.png` is the pose and scale reference for the original walking version of Bebear.
- `bebear-reference.png` is the canonical Bebear design for every new animation: white round head, blue ears, brown nose, no bear arms or body, and a blue-gray-and-cream striped comfort blanket.

Every future idle, walking, casting, guarding, hit, and story asset must use the same Coji and Bebear references. Pose and viewing angle may change; character identity, clothing, colors, and Bebear anatomy may not.
